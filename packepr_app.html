The Shopify Compliance Gap

Shopify's Weight Field Doesn't Track What the EU Regulates

Standard ecommerce workflows calculate freight based on the product. But under EU law (VerpackDG & Citeo), every single gram of secondary packaging, infill paper, and tape counts as packaging waste subject to mandatory license fees and audit reporting.

Shopify Weight ≠ Tare Weight

Shopify product weight is courier billing weight. EU law strictly requires declaring the empty shipping carton, mailer envelope, and adhesive tape tare weight separated by material fraction.

⚠ Filing product weight violates ZSVR rules

Severe Administrative Fines

Germany fines up to €200,000 under VerpackDG with sales bans on Amazon.de and Otto. France fines up to €30,000 for missing IDU declarations.

⚠ Audits triggered automatically at customs

Shopify Backend Spec (For Antigravity / Cursor)

                
Connects this UI to live Shopify Admin GraphQL & Billing APIs

              

              
            

            
Build a Shopify Remix / Next.js embedded app named "PackEPR" using Shopify Polaris and Prisma (SQLite/Supabase):
1. OAuth Scopes: read_orders, read_products, write_products.
2. Database Models:
   - BoxPreset (id, shop, name, cardboardGrams, plasticGrams, glassGrams, isDefault)
   - MerchantConfig (shop, lucidNumber, dualSystemCode, citeoIdu, planTier)
3. Order Sync Webhook & GraphQL Query:
   - Fetch orders filtered by created_at and shippingAddress.countryCodeV2 in ['DE','FR','AT','IT','ES','NL','PL','SE','BE'].
   - Multiply order count by assigned BoxPreset material weights (convert grams to kg with 3 decimals).
4. Export Endpoints:
   - /api/export/lucid-xml: Output ZSVR LUCID compliant XML with <Datenmeldung>, <Registrierungsnummer>, <Systembetreiber>, and <MaterialFraktion> (PPK, KUNSTSTOFFE, GLAS).
   - /api/export/citeo-csv: Output France Citeo / EU country summary CSV.
5. Monetization (Shopify Billing API):
   - Free Tier: Shows live country kg dashboard & threshold warnings.
   - Starter ($14/mo or $129/yr): Unlocks 1-click LUCID XML & Citeo CSV downloads.

Privacy Policy & GDPR / DSGVO Compliance

        
      

      

        
1. Data Minimisation Principle: PackEPR accesses strictly destination country codes and order creation timestamps to calculate country packaging mass. Customer names, personal phone numbers, and physical street addresses are NEVER permanently stored.

        
2. In-Memory Aggregation: Order weights are aggregated in-memory to compute kilogram totals per member state. Raw transaction records are discarded post-ledger generation.

        
3. Zero Third-Party Advertising: We never sell, rent, or monetize merchant order data. Your data is used exclusively to prepare ZSVR LUCID XML and Citeo CSV compliance filings.

        
4. EU Data Sovereignty: Servers are hosted on ISO 27001 / SOC2 compliant infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany.

      

    


    
    

      

        
Terms of Service

        
      

      

        
1. Scope of Service: PackEPR provides tare weight calculation algorithms, box preset matching, and standardized XML/CSV generation for European Extended Producer Responsibility compliance.

        
2. Accuracy of Tare Presets: The merchant is responsible for ensuring their configured empty carton, poly mailer, and tape tare weights reflect physical packaging used.

        
3. Filing Responsibility: PackEPR generates compliant data files. The merchant is legally responsible for submitting these files to their respective national registers (e.g. LUCID, Citeo).
